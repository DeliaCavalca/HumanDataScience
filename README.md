# The Incidence of Femicide in Domestic Homicides: A Statistical Analysis of 2024 Data in Italy

### Human Data Science Project


Delia Cavalca, Giuseppe Cacciapuoti, Chiara Anna Cartarasa

delia.cavalca@studio.unibo.it, giuseppe.cacciapuoti@studio.unibo.it, chiaraanna.cartarasa@studio.unibo.it

---

This study explores the phenomenon of femicide within the Italian context, focusing particularly on the year 2024. It seeks to answer the following research questions:

(1) Are women statistically more likely than men to be victims of domestic homicide?

(2) Is there a prevalent type of victim–perpetrator relationship in femicide cases?

(3) Has the number of femicides changed significantly over time between 2000 and 2024?

To address these questions, the study analyzes homicide data disaggregated by gender, with a specific focus on cases occurring in the family or affective context. The objective is to identify potential gender disparities, uncover dominant relational dynamics behind femicides, and explore long-term trends to better understand the evolution of this form of gender-based violence.
